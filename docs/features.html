Taskbar and Start menu

The taskbar

A start button, Quick Launch, window buttons, the notification area and the clock, on every monitor with that monitor's windows.

  • Grouping: windows of the same app share one button ("3 Foot ▴"); click it to pick a window.
  • Window buttons: click to show or minimise. Right-click for Restore, Minimize, Maximize and Close; middle-click closes.
  • Quick Launch: right-click a program in the Start menu, or a window button, and choose Pin to Quick Launch. Drag buttons and icons to rearrange them.
  • Settings → Taskbar (or right-click an empty part of the taskbar): auto-hide, grouping, Quick Launch and seconds on the clock.

firox-panel (GTK, layer-shell, window list over wlr-foreign-toplevel-management). Styled by the theme's panel.css; your own rules go in ~/.config/firox/panel.css. waybar is kept as a fallback.

The Start menu

  • Top: your account picture and name.
  • Left column: pinned Internet, Web Apps and Android Apps, your most-used programs, and All Programs, grouped into folders. Start typing to search.
  • Right column: Documents, Pictures, Music, Computer, Network Places, Settings, Bazaar (the app store), Help, Search and Run.
  • Bottom: Log Off and Turn Off Computer dialogs.

firox-startmenu (Python, GTK3, gtk-layer-shell)

Volume, battery and the clock

  • Speaker: click for a volume slider with Mute and a choice of speakers or headphones.
  • Battery: click for the charge and time left, battery health, a brightness slider and the power mode (Power saver, Balanced or Performance). Right-click it for Power Options.
  • Clock: click for a month calendar; right-click it for Date and Time settings.
  • Volume and brightness keys: a small level pop-up appears at the bottom of the screen.

firox-volume, firox-battery (power modes via tuned-ppd), firox-calendar, firox-osd

Notifications

Notifications pop up as small "balloons".

  • The bell in the notification area opens your last 100 notifications; click one to open its app.
  • Do Not Disturb (in the panel, or right-click the bell) holds pop-ups except urgent ones.
  • Pop-ups also wait while a screen saver runs.

mako and firox-notifications

Run

Start → Run… or Super+R: type a program, a folder, a file, a web address or any command.

  • As you type: the rest of a matching name fills in.
  • History: it remembers your last 26 entries.
  • Terminal: "Run in a terminal window" shows a command's output.
  • Old names: Windows names work too: cmd, notepad, calc, control, taskmgr, msconfig…

firox-run

Network and devices

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Both have their own pop-up on the taskbar.

  • Network icon: a Wi-Fi switch and the networks in range, strongest first. Click one to connect, and type its password right there. Wired, VPNs and Airplane mode are here too, plus Network Connections… for work networks that need a username or certificate.
  • Bluetooth icon: your devices, with battery levels, to connect, disconnect or remove. Add a device… finds new ones; a small window asks you to compare or type a pairing code.

firox-network (NetworkManager's libnm), firox-bluetooth (BlueZ, with its own pairing agent)

Mouse, touchpad and keyboard

  • Settings → Mouse: pointer speed, left-handed buttons, double-click speed, pointer size, scrolling. Its Touchpad tab covers tap to click, two-finger scrolling, the right-click method, ignore while typing, and off when a mouse is plugged in.
  • Settings → Keyboard: repeat speed, Num Lock at login, and keyboard layouts with a shortcut to switch between them.

firox-input, stored in ~/.config/firox/input.conf. A personal labwc rc.xml still wins.

The desktop

Wallpaper on every monitor and the classic icons (Computer, your home folder, Network, Recycle Bin and your Desktop folder's files) on the main display. Drag icons where you like, onto folders or the Recycle Bin; rename with F2; right-click for Arrange Icons, New Folder and more. Settings → Desktop Background picks the wallpaper, its position and colour.

firox-desktop (GTK, layer-shell)

Displays

Settings → Display arranges your monitors and saves the layout for each set of connected screens. It's re-applied at login and when you plug a monitor in.

With several monitors, choose your main display: it has the full taskbar, the Sidebar and notifications, while the others extend the desktop with a taskbar of their own windows. Identify shows which monitor is which.

firox-display, wdisplays, kanshi, firox-displays, firox_monitors

Power saving

Settings → Power Options: lock the screen, turn off the monitor and sleep after set idle times, with separate Plugged in and On battery times on laptops. Laptops warn at 10% and 5% battery, then sleep at 3%. Videos and full-screen apps keep the screen on.

firox-power; system defaults in /etc/firox/power.conf

Everyday tools

Snipping Tool

Start → All Programs, or Super+Shift+S.

  • What to snip: a rectangle, the whole screen or one monitor, with an optional delay.
  • Afterwards: each snip is copied straight away. Mark it up with a pen or highlighter, then copy or save it.
  • Print Screen still saves the whole screen to Pictures.

firox-snip

Clipboard history

  • What you copy stays available after the app you copied it from closes.
  • Super+V shows your clipboard history, with search and images.
  • Passwords that password managers mark as sensitive are never kept, and the history is cleared when you log off.

firox-clipboard (cliphist). Settings → Clipboard sets the size or turns it off.

Desktop Gadgets

A Sidebar on the right of the desktop with a Clock, Calendar, CPU Meter, Weather (no account needed; set your city in its options), a Slide Show of your Pictures, Notes, Now Playing (with play/pause for your music), a Network Meter, Feed Headlines, a Timer, a Picture Puzzle, Currency rates and Disk Space.

  • + Gadgets adds more.
  • Right-click a gadget for its options.

firox-gadgets; weather from Open-Meteo. Turn it off in Settings → Taskbar.

Task Manager

Ctrl+Alt+Del or Ctrl+Shift+Esc.

  • Applications: switch to or end a program.
  • Processes: CPU and memory for each process.
  • Performance: green-on-black graphs of CPU and memory use.

btop is in the Start menu too, as Resource Monitor.

firox-taskmgr

Screen savers

Settings → Screen Saver: Aquarium, Matrix, Bonsai, Bouncing firox, Starfield or Blank, full screen on every monitor, with Preview.

They're off by default. Add your own.

firox-screensaver (asciiquarium, cmatrix, cbonsai and more)

Alt+Tab with previews

Alt+Tab switches windows with live previews.

Also included

A Command Prompt (xfce4-terminal, with tabs; Super+Enter), Media Player (VLC, with full codecs), Notepad, Calculator and a picture viewer.

zenity is included for dialogs in shell scripts.

Updates, restore and backup

Automatic updates

Updates download in the background and apply at the next restart.

  • Waiting update: an icon appears in the notification area. Click it to restart, or right-click to see what changes.
  • Afterwards: "Firox Linux was updated" tells you once it's done.

firox-updates, rpm-ostree

System Restore

Lists the versions of Firox installed on your computer.

  • Restore goes back to the previous one, with Undo.
  • Keep holds a version even after later updates.
  • Other options: see what changed between versions, cancel a waiting update, or pause automatic updates.

Your files, settings and apps aren't affected.

firox-restore (rpm-ostree rollback, ostree admin pin)

Backup and Restore

Backs up your home folder every hour to a USB drive or a network folder, and as soon as the backup drive is plugged in.

  • Each backup is a dated folder with a complete copy, but unchanged files aren't stored twice.
  • What's kept: hourly backups for a day, daily for a month, then weekly.
  • Restore files from any date, or everything.

firox-backup (rsync --link-dest, like the original Time Machine). FAT/exFAT drives and most network shares get a Latest copy plus Older versions.

Sync between your computers

Firox computers on the same network find each other.

  • Pairing: with a 6-digit PIN shown on the other computer. The PIN never crosses the network.
  • What syncs: the folders you tick (Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos, Desktop) stay the same on all of them.
  • Older versions: replaced or deleted files are kept for 30 days.

firox-sync: Avahi discovery and a private, local-network-only Syncthing

Settings and login

Settings

The Control Panel, in alphabetical order. It covers:

  • Display, sound, network, Bluetooth, printers and disks
  • Web Apps, Android and the Bazaar app store
  • Date and Time, Taskbar and Default Programs (browser, email, music, pictures and more)
  • User Accounts (your picture and password), Startup Programs, System Restore, Backup and Sync

firox-control-panel

Startup Programs

Tick what starts when you log in: any Start menu program or your own command. Apps' own “start at login” options show up here too. Also reachable by typing msconfig in Run.

firox-startup, using standard ~/.config/autostart entries

Login screen

“To begin, click your user name”, with everyone's account picture. Other user… covers accounts that aren't listed, and Turn off computer offers sleep, turn off and restart. It uses the keyboard layout chosen when installing.

firox-welcome, a greetd greeter

Windows

Title bars with minimise, maximise and close buttons in each look's style. Drag windows to the screen edges to snap them, and press Alt+Space for the window menu.

labwc (wlroots, Openbox-style)
